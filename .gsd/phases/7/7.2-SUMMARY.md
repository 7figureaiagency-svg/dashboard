## 1. Executive Summary

Plan 7.2 delivers comprehensive UI/UX clarity and operational tools for sales executives handling portal leads that arrive with missing, masked, or landline phone numbers. Adhering strictly to Apple Human Interface Guidelines (HIG), zero additional runtime JavaScript packages, and the 100% Non-n8n architecture, sales teams now have immediate visibility and a 1-tap manual phone acquisition workflow.

When a portal inquiry arrives without a valid WhatsApp mobile number, it is automatically badged with an Apple-grade `NoWhatsAppBadge`, surfaced in the `MISSING_PHONE` quick triage filter pill with real-time counters, highlighted with a contextual alert banner in the `LeadDrawer`, and accompanied by a 1-tap modal dialog to input and validate a 10-digit Indian mobile number. Saving the phone number immediately updates Supabase, enables the automated WhatsApp AI bot, and unlocks native 44pt "Call Client" and "WhatsApp" action buttons reactively.
