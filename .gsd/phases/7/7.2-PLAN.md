# Plan 7.2: Missing WhatsApp Indicator, CRM Leads Badge/Alert & 1-Tap Manual Phone Entry

## Objective
Provide sales executives with immediate visual clarity and an actionable workflow for portal inquiries that arrive without a valid WhatsApp mobile number (masked, landline, or omitted). Build an Apple HIG `NoWhatsAppBadge`, a quick triage filter pill in `LeadsTab`, a contextual alert banner in `LeadDrawer`, and a 1-tap "Add WhatsApp Number" modal that updates the lead and instantly activates WhatsApp and calling capabilities.
