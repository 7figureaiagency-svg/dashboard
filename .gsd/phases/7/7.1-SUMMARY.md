## 1. Executive Summary

Plan 7.1 successfully establishes an autonomous, 100% Non-n8n lead ingestion pipeline for all major Indian real estate portals into the NextGen Realty CRM and Supabase Edge Function (`realtor-lead-engine`). Inbound webhooks from 99acres, MagicBricks, Housing.com, CommonFloor, Facebook Lead Ads, OLX, and custom endpoints are dynamically ingested, sanitized, and normalized. 

A 24-hour deduplication window prevents duplicate inquiries from fragmenting the CRM database. Inquiries with valid mobile numbers are matched against active properties via a 4-tier inventory auto-matcher and receive immediate consultative Hinglish WhatsApp outreach with brochure PDF and Google Maps links. The lead state transitions into `SITE_VISIT_PROPOSED`, enabling incoming customer replies to naturally continue into the TypeChat FSM conversational site visit booking flow.
