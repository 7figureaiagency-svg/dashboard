# Phase 22 Plan 22.1 Summary: State-Conditioned Multilingual Intent Classification Engine

## 1. Overview & Objectives
Phase 22 Plan 22.1 delivers an advanced, state-conditioned multilingual intent classification engine for `supabase/functions/realtor-lead-engine/index.ts`. It resolves critical conversational boundary issues where leads responding with phrased negations (e.g. "nahi karni", "dusri nahi hai", "visit nahi ho paayi", "nathi karvi") were erroneously flagged as `OPT_OUT` by generic LLM classifications, prematurely terminating high-intent lead journeys.

Key improvements implemented:
1. **Centralized Outbound Prompt Tracking & Context Injection**: Persists `lead.metadata.last_bot_prompt` atomically across interactive conversation turns and background cron sweeps (Modules 1A, 1, 2, 2B, 2C, 3, 4), and injects it into `buildLeadContextForClassifier` with a 72-hour decay threshold.
2. **Intent Precedence Hierarchy & Anti-OPT_OUT Guard Inversion**: Enforces a strict two-tier intent hierarchy (Tier 1 Autonomous Global vs. Tier 2 Contextual Answers) and inverts the `OPT_OUT` guard so that `OPT_OUT` is strictly impossible unless `isExplicitOptOut === true`.
3. **Dialect & Negation Safety**: Recovers conversational negations contextually (e.g. `OWNER_DETAILS_GATHERED` negations -> `GENERAL_QUERY` / decline second listing without dropping; `SITE_VISIT_BOOKED` negations -> `CANNOT_ATTEND_VISIT`; `CRITERIA_CONFIRMATION_PENDING` negations -> `CONFIRM_CRITERIA` with `confirm: false`).
