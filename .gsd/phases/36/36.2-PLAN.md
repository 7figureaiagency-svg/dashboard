# Plan 36.2: Owner Submission Plot Area Sync, Field Parity & Inspector Drawer Polish

<objective>
Ensure plot submissions map `plot_area_sqft` accurately to inventory upon approval in `src/lib/crm-data.ts`, achieve 100% field parity between `EditOwnerSubmissionDialog` and `AddInventoryDialog`, and fix alignment issues in the submission inspection drawer across both mobile and desktop.
</objective>

<context>
- In `src/lib/crm-data.ts` (`useApprovePropertySubmission`), `areaVal` checks `carpet_area_sqft`, `super_built_up_sqft`, and `rawAttrs.area_sqft`, but ignores `submission.plot_area_sqft`. This causes approved plot listings to have `null` area in inventory unless manually filled in attributes.
- In `src/components/crm/EditOwnerSubmissionDialog.tsx`, several fields available in `AddInventoryDialog.tsx` (`maintenance_fees`, `furnishing_status`, `facing`, `floor_number`, `total_floors`, `age_of_property`, `parking_spaces`, `possession_status`, `verified_by_agency`) are missing or uneditable prior to approval.
- In `src/components/crm/OwnerSubmissionsTab.tsx`, the inspection view (eye icon) has alignment quirks on mobile and narrow browser viewports.
</context>

<tasks>
<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 1: Fix Plot Area & PropTech Attributes Mapping in useApprovePropertySubmission</name>
  <files>
    src/lib/crm-data.ts
  </files>
  <action>
    1. In `useApprovePropertySubmission`:
       - Update `areaVal` resolution:
         ```typescript
         const areaVal = submission.carpet_area_sqft
           ? Number(submission.carpet_area_sqft)
           : submission.super_built_up_sqft
           ? Number(submission.super_built_up_sqft)
           : submission.plot_area_sqft
           ? Number(submission.plot_area_sqft)
           : rawAttrs.area_sqft
           ? Number(rawAttrs.area_sqft)
           : null;
         ```
       - Include `plot_area_sqft` explicitly:
         `plot_area_sqft: submission.plot_area_sqft ? Number(submission.plot_area_sqft) : null,`
       - Ensure all attributes in `rawAttrs` (`furnishing_status`, `facing`, `floor_number`, `total_floors`, `age_of_property`, `parking_spaces`, `possession_status`, `maintenance_fees`, `verified_by_agency`) are preserved and merged into the inventory insert record.
  </action>
  <verify>
    Write a test script `scratch/test_owner_approval_mapping.cjs` checking that:
    - If submission has `plot_area_sqft: 1500`, `areaVal` is 1500.
    - Extended attributes (`furnishing_status`, `facing`, `parking_spaces`) are present in attributes payload.
  </verify>
  <done>
    Plot submissions and rich property attributes are preserved 100% when converting owner submissions to inventory.
  </done>
</task>
