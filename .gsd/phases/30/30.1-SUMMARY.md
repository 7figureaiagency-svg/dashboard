# Phase 30.1 Summary: Vernacular Seller Entity Extraction & Adjective Stop-Word Guard

## Objective Achieved
Eliminated the critical bug where vernacular seller adjectives and intent verb conjugations (e.g. *"ek maro juno flat vechvano che"*) caused the bot to falsely extract `"Juno Vechvano"` as a project/society/location name. Expanded `STOP_WORDS_SET` with exhaustive Gujarati, Hindi, and English real estate adjectives and seller verb conjugations, strengthened `isLikelySocietyOrProjectName` to reject descriptive modifiers and pure city localities, upgraded `extractProjectOrSocietyFromText`, and ensured `handleOwnerFlow` cleanly routes to society and locality inquiry without polluting draft metadata with hallucinated property names.

## Key Changes Implemented

### 1. Stop-Word Dictionary Expansion (`STOP_WORDS_SET`)
- Added comprehensive Gujarati adjectives: `juno`, `juni`, `junu`, `juna`, `navo`, `navi`, `navu`, `nava`, `maro`, `mari`, `maru`, `mara`, `taro`, `tari`, `taru`, `tara`, `tamaro`, `tamari`, `tamaru`, `tamara`, `amaro`, `amari`, `amaru`, `amara`, `potano`, `potani`, `potanu`, `potana`.
- Added Hindi adjectives: `purana`, `purani`, `purane`, `purano`, `naya`, `nayi`, `naye`, `apna`, `apni`, `apne`, `mera`, `meri`, `mere`, `hamara`, `hamari`, `hamare`.
- Added English & common adjectives: `old`, `new`, `used`, `resale`, `re-sale`, `secondhand`, `second-hand`, `fresh`.
- Added Gujarati verb conjugations: `vechvano`, `vechvani`, `vechvanu`, `vechvana`, `vechavo`, `vechavi`, `vechavu`, `vechava`, `vechvu`, `vechva`, `vechvo`, `aapo`, `aapva`, `aapvu`, `aapvani`, `aapvano`.
- Added Hindi verb conjugations: `bechvano`, `bechvani`, `bechvanu`, `bechvana`, `bechne`, `bechna`, `bechni`, `dena`, `deni`, `dene`.
- Added Gujarati and Hindi Unicode equivalents (`જૂનો`, `જૂની`, `જૂનું`, `જૂના`, `વેચવાનો`, `વેચવાની`, `વેચવાનું`, `વેચવાના`, `વેચવું`, `વેચવા`, `पुराना`, `पुरानी`, `पुराने`, `नया`, `नयी`, `नए`, `बेचना`, `बेचनी`).

### 2. Strengthened Society & Project Name Guard (`isLikelySocietyOrProjectName`)
- Stripped punctuation (`['".,!?;:()]`) to normalize tokens cleanly.
- Added regex guard rejecting descriptive modifier + property type + verb combinations (`/^(?:juno|juni|junu|juna|purana|purani|purane|naya|nayi|naye|navo|navi|navu|nava|resale|old|new|used)\s*(?:flat|flats|villa|house|plot|shop|office|makan|ghar)?(?:\s*(?:vechvano|vechvani|vechvanu|vechvana|vechva|vechvu|bechna|bechni|bechne|bechvano|sell))?$/i`).
- Added locality protection guard: if `cleaned` is a known city locality (`extractLocationFromText(cleaned)` or `GUJARATI_HINDI_LOCATION_MAP`), it is strictly rejected as a society name.

### 3. Step 5 Entity Extraction Token Stripping (`extractProjectOrSocietyFromText`)
- Added all new adjectives and seller verb conjugations to Step 5 regex stripping.
- Enforced minimum length check (`stripped.length >= 3`) and `isLikelySocietyOrProjectName(stripped)`.
- Input `"ek maro juno flat vechvano che"` now cleanly resolves to empty string `""`.

### 4. Owner Flow Intake, State Routing & Locality Disambiguation (`handleOwnerFlow`)
- When entering owner flow with `"ek maro juno flat vechvano che"`, correctly sets `draft.property_type = "Flat"`, `draft.transaction_type = "SELL"`, and keeps `draft.society_name = null` and `draft.location = null`.
- Turn 2 greeting (`OWNER_SOCIETY_PENDING`):
  - Gujlish: `Bahuj saras! <b>Flat</b> note kari lidhu chhe. 👍\nAa property kaya <b>Project ke Society</b> ma aaveli chhe? (e.g. Nilamber Bellissimo, Sun Crest) 🏢`
  - Hinglish: `Bahut badhiya! <b>Flat</b> note kar liya hai. 👍\nYe property kaunse <b>Project ya Society</b> me hai? (e.g. Nilamber Bellissimo, Sun Crest) 🏢`
  - English: `Great! We have noted your <b>Flat</b>. 👍\nWhich <b>Project or Society</b> is this property located in? (e.g. Nilamber Bellissimo, Sun Crest) 🏢`
- Turn 2 locality detection:
  - If user replies with locality like `"waghodia"` during `OWNER_SOCIETY_PENDING`, normalizes and sets `draft.location = "Waghodia Road"`, `draft.society_name = "Standalone / Independent"`.
  - Seamlessly skips redundant Turn 3 locality question and advances directly to `OWNER_AREA_PENDING` (Carpet Area + Floor).
- Turn 3 (`OWNER_LOCATION_PENDING`):
  - Normalizes locality responses using `extractLocationFromText` (e.g. `"waghodia"` -> `"Waghodia Road"`).
  - Cleaned acknowledgment prefix (omits `Noted Society!` or `Noted Standalone!`).
- Router entry points (`FRESH`, `ROLE_SELECTION_PENDING`, `parseRequirementFromText`):
  - Updated seller action patterns to detect `vechvano`, `vechvani`, `bechvano`, `bechvani` even when separated by descriptive adjectives (`"maro juno flat vechvano che"`).
