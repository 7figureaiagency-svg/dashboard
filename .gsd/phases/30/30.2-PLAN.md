# Plan 30.2: Compound Question Disambiguation & Remaining-Question Recovery Engine

## Objective
Enforce the strict user rule: "ek sath do sawal puche and ek ka hi jawab mile to next me remaining question firse pucho" (When two questions are asked in a compound turn and the user only answers one, ask the remaining question in the next step before moving forward). Implement dedicated remaining-question recovery for Area + Floor (`OWNER_AREA_PENDING` -> `OWNER_FLOOR_PENDING`), Price + Furnishing (`OWNER_PRICE_PENDING` -> `OWNER_FURNISHING_PENDING`), and buyer qualification compound fields, preventing critical property attributes from being skipped or defaulted to wrong values.

## Context
- `supabase/functions/realtor-lead-engine/index.ts`:
  - `DialogState` type definition (around line 5810): Add `OWNER_FLOOR_PENDING` to track pending floor inquiries.
  - `Turn 4 in handleOwnerFlow` (lines 5337-5412): Currently asks `Carpet Area ketlo chhe ane kaya Floor par chhe?`. When user answers `1250`, `hasArea` becomes true and the floor question is discarded, jumping straight to Turn 5.
  - `Turn 5 in handleOwnerFlow` (lines 5414-5500): Currently asks `Expected Selling Price ketli chhe, ane property Furnished, Semi ke Unfurnished chhe?`. When user answers `40 lakh ma`, price is saved but furnishing is skipped and defaulted.
  - `handleQualificationCascade` (Seeker qualification): Ensure that when compound questions are presented, single-entity responses prompt for the remaining missing entity in the very next turn.
