# Plan 30.1: Vernacular Seller Entity Extraction & Adjective Stop-Word Guard

## Objective
Fix the critical bug where vernacular adjectives and seller intent verbs (e.g. "ek maro juno flat vechvano che") cause the bot to falsely extract "Juno Vechvano" as a project/society/location name. Expand `STOP_WORDS_SET` with exhaustive Gujarati/Hindi/English real estate adjectives and seller verb conjugations, upgrade `extractProjectOrSocietyFromText` and `isLikelySocietyOrProjectName` to reject all descriptive modifiers, and ensure `handleOwnerFlow` cleanly routes to project/society and locality inquiry without polluting draft metadata with hallucinated property names.

## Context
- `supabase/functions/realtor-lead-engine/index.ts`:
  - `STOP_WORDS_SET` (around line 1378): Missing `juno`, `juni`, `junu`, `juna`, `purana`, `purani`, `purane`, `vechvano`, `vechvani`, `vechvanu`, `vechvana`, `bechvano`, `bechvani`, `bechvanu`, `bechvana`, `resale`, `secondhand`, `navo`, `navi`, etc.
  - `isLikelySocietyOrProjectName` (around line 1394): Evaluates whether a candidate name is a valid society/project name. Must reject any phrase composed solely of adjectives, intent verbs, and unit configurations.
  - `extractProjectOrSocietyFromText` (around line 1412): Step 5 token stripping missed `vechvano`, `juno`, leaving `"juno vechvano"`.
  - `handleOwnerFlow` (around lines 4687-4693 and 4823-4832): Sets `draft.society_name` and `draft.project_name` from regex extraction. If no genuine society name exists in the utterance, both must remain `null` or empty.
  - `Turn 2 / Turn 3 in handleOwnerFlow` (lines 5250-5335): Handles society name and exact locality (`OWNER_SOCIETY_PENDING` -> `OWNER_LOCATION_PENDING`).
