## 1. Executive Summary

Plan 30.2 successfully addressed and eliminated conversational information leakage during seller listing intake. Specifically, it resolves the scenario where a compound question asks for two fields (e.g. Carpet Area + Floor, or Expected Price + Furnishing), and the user only replies with one entity (e.g. `1250` or `40 lakh ma`).

Previously, the FSM would advance to the subsequent turn and default the unanswered field (causing floors to be missed entirely and furnishing to be silently defaulted). With Plan 30.2, the system strictly enforces the recovery protocol:
- If Area is provided without Floor on multi-story properties, the engine transitions to `OWNER_FLOOR_PENDING` and asks the remaining floor question.
- If Price/Rent is provided without Furnishing status, the engine transitions to `OWNER_FURNISHING_PENDING` and prompts for Furnishing status.
- Final summary and database persistence cleanly format project name, location, and floor (e.g., `Flat in Waghodia Road (4th Floor)`), eliminating generic placeholders such as "Standalone / Independent" or "Society".
