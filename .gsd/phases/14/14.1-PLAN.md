# Plan 14.1: Desktop Horizontal Segmented Tab Ribbon & Full-Width Viewport Expansion

<objective>
Transform the CRM dashboard layout from a 240px vertical left sidebar into a sleek, Apple-grade horizontal segmented tab ribbon positioned at the top in the sticky header. Reclaim the left-side space so sales executives and admins can view full lead details, contact info, property criteria, and status badges without cramped columns or horizontal scrolling.

Purpose: Solve the user's issue where the vertical left sidebar wastes 240px of screen real-estate, causing the Leads CRM and Live Inventory tables to feel constricted on desktop.
Output: Refactored `src/routes/_authenticated/dashboard.tsx` with horizontal top navigation and wide-screen layout.
</objective>
