<objective>
Fix the issue where Manthan's booked site visit lead in Griham Realty fails to appear on the Pipeline Board.
Root causes:
1. `hydrateLead` (`src/lib/crm.ts`) mapped `status` strictly from `raw.state`. When Manthan asked an office FAQ question earlier, the FAQ interceptor in `realtor-lead-engine` set `state = "ROLE_SELECTION_PENDING"`.

3. Furthermore, intermediate states like `ROLE_SELECTION_PENDING`, `OPTIONS_SENT`, and `DUAL_ROLE_MENU_PENDING` were completely omitted from all 6 pipeline columns, causing leads in those states to fall into a vacuum where they did not appear in any stage (even while the header said "Showing 1 lead").

Implement exhaustive pipeline stage mapping, resilient site visit hydration, and non-destructive FAQ state preservation.
</objective>
