### 1. Multi-Dialect Automated Simulation Matrix (`scratch/test_phase27_conversational_intelligence.cjs`)
- **Prompt Token Footprint:**
  - Base Core System Prompt: ~630 tokens (2,520 characters).
  - Micro-Prompt Max Size: ~350 tokens (1,400 characters).
  - Total turn prompt: **~980 tokens** (reduced by **54%** from legacy 2,150+ tokens).
- **Numeric Disambiguation & Token Tree Collisions:**
  - `"2 BHK"` in `ROLE_SELECTION_PENDING` -> strictly classified as `CHANGE_REQUIREMENT` (user_role: "SEEKER"), never Option 2 (`SUBMIT_LISTING`).
  - Isolated `"2"` or `"2️⃣"` in `ROLE_SELECTION_PENDING` -> strictly classified as Option 2 (`SUBMIT_LISTING` / OWNER).
  - Isolated `"1"` or `"1️⃣"` in `ROLE_SELECTION_PENDING` -> strictly classified as Option 1 (`CHANGE_REQUIREMENT` / SEEKER).
  - `"1"` in `OWNER_POSSESSION_PENDING` -> `READY_TO_MOVE`.
  - `"2"` in `OWNER_POSSESSION_PENDING` -> `UNDER_CONSTRUCTION`.
  - `"1"`, `"2"`, `"3"` in `OWNER_FURNISHING_PENDING` -> `UNFURNISHED`, `SEMI_FURNISHED`, `FURNISHED`.
  - `"1"` vs `"2"` in post-visit attendance survey -> `CONFIRM_VISIT_ATTENDANCE` (attended: true) vs `CANNOT_ATTEND_VISIT` (attended: false).
- **Indic & Gujarati Vernacular Dialects:**
  - `"Bhade flat vadodra maa reva mate"` -> `requirement: "RENT"`.
  - `"Kiraye par office chahiye"` -> `requirement: "LEASE" / "RENT"`.
  - Indic Gujarati numerals (`૧`, `૨`, `૩`) normalize cleanly to ASCII digits (`1`, `2`, `3`) without failing tree evaluation.
- **Conversational Detour Interception & Resumption:**
  - FAQ query during `OWNER_PRICE_PENDING` yields dual-payload (Address answer + resumed price prompt) in 1 turn while preserving `owner_draft` and keeping state as `OWNER_PRICE_PENDING`.
- **Multi-Entity Archival & Multi-Search:**

  - Secondary search preserves active booked visit timestamp and records previous search in `metadata.seeker_profile.searches[]`.
