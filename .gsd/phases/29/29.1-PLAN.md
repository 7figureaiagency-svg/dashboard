       - When the bot is expecting an answer to a specific qualification question (e.g. expecting "Buy" vs "Rent", or expecting Budget, or expecting Purpose) and receives unrecognized text (like user typed `"But"` or gibberish):

         - English: `Sorry, I did not quite catch that. 🙏\n👉 Are you looking to <b>Buy</b> or <b>Rent</b>?`
