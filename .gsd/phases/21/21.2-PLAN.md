# Plan 21.2: Apple HIG In-Dashboard "Property Pitch Studio" Modal & Multi-Surface Integration

<objective>
Equip sales executives and brokers with an in-dashboard "Property Pitch Studio" accessible across all property and lead surfaces:
1. Build `src/components/crm/property-pitch-modal.tsx`: an Apple HIG modal featuring segmented controls for Persona, Tone/Language, and Format, an interactive WhatsApp message preview bubble, dynamic EMI highlight, and 1-tap Copy, WhatsApp Web share, and Lead Activity audit logger.
2. Integrate the Pitch Studio across the CRM:
   - In `src/components/crm/inventory-tab.tsx`: Add **"✨ Pitch"** triggers on Live Inventory units (table rows and mobile cards) and on Owner Submissions resale cards.
   - In `src/components/crm/lead-drawer.tsx`: Add **"✨ Craft Pitch"** trigger on Top Matching Properties and Resale units so executives can instantly pitch matched properties to the inspected lead.
3. Validate compilation with `npm.cmd run build`.
</objective>

<context>
- Plan 21.1 creates the copywriting frameworks in `.agents/skills/property-pitch-crafter/SKILL.md` and the algorithmic engine in `src/lib/pitch-crafter.ts`.
- Sales executives often need to share property details with prospective clients on WhatsApp during or right after a phone call. Manually writing compelling copy with emojis, EMI calculations, and highlights takes 5-10 minutes and varies wildly in quality.
- The Property Pitch Studio eliminates this friction, allowing sales reps to generate a customized, psychology-grounded pitch in under 3 seconds.
- Invariants:
  - 100% Non-n8n (all interactions handled within CRM client and Supabase database)
  - 0 additional runtime JS packages
  - Apple HIG visual standards (squircles, translucent materials, hairline borders, 44pt touch ergonomics)
</context>
