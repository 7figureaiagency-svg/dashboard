# Phase 21 Plan 2 Summary: Apple HIG Property Pitch Studio Modal & Multi-Surface CRM Integration

## Implementation Overview
Built and wired the **Property Pitch Studio** across the entire CRM dashboard, empowering sales executives to generate tailored, psychology-grounded WhatsApp pitches in under 3 seconds for any property and prospective buyer.

### Delivered Components & Modifications
1. **`src/components/crm/property-pitch-modal.tsx` (New)**:
   - Built an Apple HIG dialog modal using translucent materials (`backdrop-blur-2xl`), continuous squircles (`rounded-3xl`), and hairline borders (`border-border/60`).
   - Integrated reactive controls:
     - **Buyer Persona**: `[ 👨‍👩‍👧 Family | 📈 Investor | 💎 Luxury | 🏷️ Starter ]` (auto-inferred from budget/requirement).
     - **Linguistic Dialect**: `[ 🇮🇳 Hinglish | 🦁 Gujlish | 🌐 English ]`.
     - **Format**: `[ ⚡ Quick Hook | 📋 Deep-Dive & EMI | 🔥 Urgent Scarcity ]`.
   - Financial intelligence strip displaying:
     - Estimated Monthly EMI (`80% LTV @ 8.5% p.a.` standard benchmark).
     - Calculated Rate per sq.ft.
     - Carpet area & Locality.
   - High-fidelity WhatsApp chat bubble live preview:
     - Shows verified outgoing message formatting, emojis, and timestamp.
     - Interactive editor: toggle between live preview and inline textarea with a 1-tap "Reset to Template" option.
   - 1-tap Action Buttons:
     - **Copy to Clipboard** with animated checkmark feedback.
     - **Send via WhatsApp** (`wa.me` or `api.whatsapp.com/send` intent).
     - Auto-logs audit record to `lead_activity` and `lead_events` (`action_type: "PITCH_SHARED"`).

2. **`src/components/crm/inventory-tab.tsx` (Modified)**:
   - Integrated `<PropertyPitchModal />` container at root.
   - Added **"✨ Pitch"** action buttons across:
     - Live Inventory Grid cards.
     - Live Inventory Mobile list cards.
     - Live Inventory Desktop table rows.
     - Live Inventory "Matching Buyers Ready" dialog rows (pitches directly to the matched buyer).
     - Owner Submissions cards and Desktop table rows.
     - Owner Submissions Details dialog footer.

3. **`src/components/crm/lead-drawer.tsx` (Modified)**:
   - Added **"✨ Pitch"** trigger to the **Top Matching Properties & Resale** cards.
   - Passes both the selected property and the inspected lead, auto-inferring the client's persona and pre-filling their phone number for instant WhatsApp outreach.
