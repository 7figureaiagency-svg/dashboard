# Plan 32.1: Commercial Seller Flow, Dynamic Role Handoff & Negation Safeguards

## Objective
1. Eliminate false "लीज़ बजट कितना है?" commercial lease seeker prompt when an owner states they want to sell commercial property (e.g. "मुझे शॉप बेचनी है मेरी").
2. Support full Devanagari Hindi, Gujarati, and Latin seller verbs and phrases (`बेचना`, `बेचनी`, `वेचવું`, `bechna`, `sell`, `resale`) in intent classification and entry routing.
3. Establish a Commercial Pricing Matrix separating Sale (Expected Selling Price / मांग), Rent (Monthly Rent), and Lease (Commercial Lease Terms) with appropriate owner vs seeker phrasing.
4. Implement dynamic mid-stream role switching from `QUALIFYING` state to `handleOwnerFlow` when an owner intent is stated, preventing seeker mode lock-in.
5. Fix conversational deadlock repetition when user negates buyer intent ("मुझे चाहिए नहीं, मुझे शॉप बेचनी है"), breaking the `isTypoOrUnrecognized` loop and transitioning cleanly.
