# Summary 32.1: Commercial Seller Flow, Dynamic Role Handoff & Negation Safeguards

## Overview
Resolved the critical conversational routing bugs in `supabase/functions/realtor-lead-engine/index.ts`:
1. Prevented commercial sellers from being misrouted into Seeker Commercial Lease flow. Devanagari Hindi phrases like "मुझे शॉप बेचनी है मेरी" now correctly parse as `requirement = "SELL"`, `property_type = "Commercial Shop"`, and route to `handleOwnerFlow`.
2. Replaced the catch-all `else` branch in `missingStage === "BUDGET"` (which blindly prompted "👉 Aapka approximate Commercial Lease budget kitna rahega?") with an explicit requirement check (`BUY` -> purchase budget, `RENT` -> monthly rent, `LEASE` -> lease budget, and safe purchase fallback).
3. Added dynamic role handoff in `dispatchStateAction` during `QUALIFYING` state via `isOwnerIntentUtterance`, allowing sellers trapped in buyer flow to switch seamlessly to owner intake.
4. Added anti-deadlock negation interceptor (`isNegationOfSeeker`) so saying "मुझे चाहिए नहीं, मुझे शॉप बेचनी है" or "नहीं चाहिए" breaks the `isTypoOrUnrecognized` loop and guides the user.
