# Plan 17.1: Portal-Exclusive Missing WhatsApp Banner & Telegram Lead Identity Isolation

<objective>
Ensure that the warning message and associated CTA "Portal inquiry me WhatsApp number provide nahi kiya gaya" / "Add WhatsApp Number" are strictly restricted to genuine portal leads (e.g. 99acres, MagicBricks, Housing, CommonFloor, OLX, Facebook Lead Ads, portal webhook imports) where a phone number is missing or masked.

Eliminate false-positive portal banners on Telegram leads (such as Manthan `5117510624` in Griham Realty) and manual leads by:
1. Creating centralized, robust `isPortalLead` and `isTelegramLead` helper predicates.
2. Fixing `hydrateLead` in `src/lib/crm.ts` so numeric Telegram sender IDs and tg_ prefixes hydrate to `source = "Telegram"` instead of defaulting to `"WhatsApp"`.
3. Restricting the amber warning banner and sticky "Add WhatsApp Number" CTA in `src/components/crm/lead-drawer.tsx` strictly to `isPortalLead(lead) && isMissingPhone`.
4. Updating `src/components/crm/leads-tab.tsx` so table rows and cards display `TG: <id>` for Telegram leads and `<NoWhatsAppBadge />` exclusively for portal leads without phone numbers.
</objective>
