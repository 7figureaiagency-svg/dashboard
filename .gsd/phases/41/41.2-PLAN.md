# Plan 41.2: Router State Guards, Visit Lifecycle & Option Selection AI Modernization

## Objective
Eradicate hardcoded regexes in visit lifecycle management (`isUnableToAttendVisit`, `isRescheduleVisit`, `isCancelVisit`, `isVisitDetails`), first-turn role intake (`ROLE_SELECTION_PENDING`), and post-options handling (`OPTIONS_SENT`, `SITE_VISIT_DONE`). Elevate `cognitiveTurn.primary_intent`, `cognitiveTurn.selected_option`, and `cognitiveTurn.active_role` as the authoritative single source of truth.

## Context
- File: `supabase/functions/realtor-lead-engine/index.ts`
- Sections:
  - Visit actions (lines 8709–8740)
  - `ROLE_SELECTION_PENDING` (lines 9090–9230)
  - `OPTIONS_SENT` & `SITE_VISIT_DONE` (lines 9550–10660)

## Tasks

### Task 1: Visit Actions Driven by AI Intent
- In lines 8709–8740:
  - `isUnableToAttendVisit`: Replace brittle regex with `cognitiveTurn?.primary_intent === "CANCEL_VISIT" || cognitiveTurn?.primary_intent === "VISIT_NOT_ATTENDED" || actionResult.intent === "CANNOT_ATTEND_VISIT"`.
  - `isRescheduleVisit`: Replace regex with `cognitiveTurn?.primary_intent === "RESCHEDULE_VISIT" || actionResult.intent === "RESCHEDULE_VISIT"`.
  - `isCancelVisit`: Replace regex with `cognitiveTurn?.primary_intent === "CANCEL_VISIT" || actionResult.intent === "CANCEL_VISIT"`.
  - `isVisitDetails`: Replace regex with `(cognitiveTurn?.selected_option === 1 && lead.metadata?.last_bot_prompt?.question_type === "VISIT_ATTENDANCE") || cognitiveTurn?.primary_intent === "ASK_FAQ"`.
  - `isSecondarySearchChoice`: Replace regex with `cognitiveTurn?.selected_option === 3 || (cognitiveTurn?.primary_intent === "SEARCH_PROPERTY" && Boolean(lead.site_visit_booked))`.

### Task 2: First-Turn Role Intake AI Modernization (`ROLE_SELECTION_PENDING`)
- In lines 9090–9230:
  - Modernize `isInitialSearchChoice`: `cognitiveTurn?.selected_option === 1 || cognitiveTurn?.active_role === "BUYER" || cognitiveTurn?.primary_intent === "SEARCH_PROPERTY"`.
  - Modernize `isInitialListChoice`: `cognitiveTurn?.selected_option === 2 || cognitiveTurn?.active_role === "SELLER" || cognitiveTurn?.primary_intent === "SUBMIT_PROPERTY"`.
  - Remove redundant `isDirectOwnerUtterance` regexes and bind to `cognitiveTurn?.primary_intent === "SUBMIT_PROPERTY"`.

### Task 3: Post-Options & Post-Visit Actions AI Modernization
- In `OPTIONS_SENT` & `SITE_VISIT_DONE`:
  - Modernize option selections using `cognitiveTurn?.selected_option` (1, 2, 3).
  - Modernize `hasAskedOptions` / `isAlternativeRequest` using `cognitiveTurn?.primary_intent === "SEARCH_PROPERTY"`.
  - Modernize `isAdvisorCallRequest` using `cognitiveTurn?.primary_intent === "REQUEST_CALL"`.
  - Modernize feedback objections using `Boolean(cognitiveTurn?.side_question) || cognitiveTurn?.primary_intent === "ASK_FAQ"`.
