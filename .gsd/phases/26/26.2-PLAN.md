  <name>Smart 6-Turn Compound Intake with Deadlock-Free Partial Extraction (F-03 Fix)</name>
  <files>supabase/functions/realtor-lead-engine/index.ts</files>
  <action>
    Restructure `handleOwnerFlow` steps into 6 clear, compound stages with Primary Mandate vs Secondary Enrichment logic:
    1. **Turn 1 (Type & Transaction):**
       - Prompt: `Property kis type ki hai — Flat, Villa ya Shop/Office? Aur Sell karni hai ya Rent/Lease par deni hai? 🏢`
       - Captures: `property_category`, `property_type`, `configuration`, `transaction_type` (`SELL`, `RENT_OUT`, `LEASE_OUT`).
    2. **Turn 2 (Project / Complex Name):**
       - Residential Prompt: `Project ya Society ka kya naam hai? (e.g. Nilamber Bellissimo, Sun Crest) 🏢`
       - Commercial Prompt: `Commercial Complex, Business Hub ya Road ka kya naam hai? (e.g. Atlantis K-10, Sayaji Plaza) 🏢`
       - Plot Prompt: `Scheme / Township ka naam ya Survey number kya hai? 📍`
    3. **Turn 3 (Exact Locality):**
       - Prompt: `Ye kaunse exact Area ya Landmark ke paas hai? 📍`
    4. **Turn 4 (Area + Floor Compound Question - F-03 Fix):**
       - For Flats/Offices: `Approximate Carpet Area kitna hai aur kaunse Floor par hai? (e.g. 1450 sq.ft, 4th floor) 📐`
       - Upgraded parser: Extracts `carpet_area_sqft` / `super_built_up_sqft` AND `floor_number` + `total_floors`.
       - **F-03 Deadlock Prevention**: Area is the Mandatory Primary Field. Floor is Secondary. If Area is extracted (even if floor is missing), advance to Turn 5! Never loop or repeat Turn 4 just because floor was not mentioned.
    5. **Turn 5 (Price/Rent + Furnishing Compound Question - F-03 Fix):**
       - For Sale: `Expected Selling Price kitna hai, aur property Furnished, Semi ya Unfurnished hai? 💰`
       - For Rent/Lease: `Expected Monthly Rent/Lease kitna hai, aur Furnishing status kya hai? 💰`
       - Upgraded parser: Extracts `expected_price` / `expected_rent` AND `furnishing_status`.
       - **F-03 Deadlock Prevention**: Price/Rent is the Mandatory Primary Field. Furnishing is Secondary. If Price/Rent is extracted (even if furnishing is missing), advance to Turn 6. Furnishing defaults to `"SEMI_FURNISHED"`.
    6. **Turn 6 (Parking + Possession + Calling Number):**
       - Prompt: `Car parking available hai? Aur property Ready to Move hai ya Under Construction? (Sath me direct calling number confirm karein) 🚗🔑`
       - Extracts `parking` ("1 Covered", "Open", etc.), `possession_status` (`READY_TO_MOVE`, `UNDER_CONSTRUCTION`), and `contact_phone`.
    AVOID: Asking 12 separate single-field questions that cause user drop-off, and AVOID requiring both compound fields to advance.
  </action>
