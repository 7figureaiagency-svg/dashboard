## 1. Overview & Objectives

Plan 26.1 transforms the conversational entry-point into an ultra-short (<= 3 lines), 100% city-agnostic system across natural Gujlish, Hinglish, and English with zero hardcoded city names. It resolves two critical FSM bugs and delivers frictionless multi-persona onboarding:
1. **Ultra-Short 3-Choice Triage (F-10 Mitigation):** Initial greetings now present a horizontal emoji-pill prompt:
   - 1️⃣ Buy/Rent (Levi chhe / Leni hai)
   - 2️⃣ Sell/Lease (Aapvi chhe / Deni hai)
   - 3️⃣ Both/Upgrade (Banne / Dono)
   in strictly <= 3 lines with zero hardcoded "Vadodara" references.
2. **F-02 Greeting Trap Fix:** Intercepts `GREETING` messages in `ROLE_SELECTION_PENDING` so saying "Hi" or "Namaste" re-prompts the 3-choice menu instead of silently assuming Seeker qualification.
3. **F-01 Dual-Role Initial vs Returning Disambiguation:** Differentiates fresh Option 3 leads (`is_initial_dual_onboarding: true`) from returning dual-role clients. A fresh lead replying "1" lists their property first (`OWNER_CONFIG_PENDING`, `active_persona = SELLER`), while replying "2" searches new options first (`QUALIFYING`, `active_persona = BUYER`), eliminating the deadlock.
4. **Direct Intent Auto-Bypass:** Explicit buyer requirements (e.g. "3bhk flat lena hai") and owner listings ("shop bechni he") in `FRESH` or `ROLE_SELECTION_PENDING` immediately skip the 1/2/3 menu and route directly to qualification or owner intake.
5. **Contextual Returning Lead Briefings:** Returning leads who greet with an active visit receive a compact scheduled visit card with 4 action shortcuts (1. Details, 2. Reschedule/Cancel, 3. Other Search, 4. List Property). Leads with listed properties receive a 4-action listing status card.

## 2. Key Code Changes

### A. Universal City-Agnostic Triage Helper (`supabase/functions/realtor-lead-engine/index.ts`)
- Added `getGreetingTriageReply(lang, nameStr, agencyName)` generating ultra-short 3-line horizontal pill options across Gujlish, Hinglish, and English.
- Purged all hardcoded references to `"Vadodara"` in initial greetings, visit snippets, and property label templates, using dynamic `${agencyName}` and `${displayCity}`.
- Replaced wordy greetings in `FRESH`, `ROLE_SELECTION_PENDING`, and catch-all fallbacks with `getGreetingTriageReply`.

### B. Greeting Guard & Auto-Bypass (`ROLE_SELECTION_PENDING` & `FRESH`)
- Added `isGreetingInRole` guard: Re-prompts 3-choice menu and maintains `nextState: "ROLE_SELECTION_PENDING"`.
- Handled Option 3 (`isBothChoice`): Sets `is_initial_dual_onboarding: true`, transitions to `DUAL_ROLE_MENU_PENDING` with initial dual-onboarding choice.
- Handled Option 2 (`isOwnerChoice`): Routes to `handleOwnerFlow` or `OWNER_CONFIG_PENDING`.
- Handled Option 1 (`isSeekerChoice`): Transitions to `QUALIFYING` with `lead_type = "SEEKER"`.
- Auto-Bypass: Evaluates `hasBuyerDirectCriteria` and `isOwnerDirectIntent` to jump directly to `handleQualificationCascade` or `handleOwnerFlow`.

### C. Initial vs Returning Dual-Role Disambiguation (`DUAL_ROLE_MENU_PENDING`)
- Checked `isInitialDualOnboarding = Boolean(lead.metadata?.is_initial_dual_onboarding || (!lead.metadata?.has_seller_profile && !lead.metadata?.submission_id && !lead.site_visit_booked))`.
- If true:
  - Option 1 ("1", "list", "bechna"): Sets `active_persona = "SELLER"` and advances to `OWNER_CONFIG_PENDING`, clearing the flag.
  - Option 2 ("2", "search", "buy", "naye"): Sets `active_persona = "BUYER"` and advances to `QUALIFYING`, clearing the flag.
- If false: Preserves returning dual-role status menu (1. Site Visit / Search, 2. Listing Status, 3. Senior Advisor Call).

### D. Compact Returning Briefings (Section 1b)
- Leads with active visits receive compact scheduled visit cards with Google Maps hyperlinks and 4 shortcuts.
- Leads with property submissions receive listed property cards with review status and 4 shortcuts.
- Added Option 4 (Property List) handling in `SITE_VISIT_BOOKED`.
