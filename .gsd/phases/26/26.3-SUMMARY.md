## Executive Summary

Phase 26.3 transformed the buyer qualification and multi-property inquiry lifecycle in `supabase/functions/realtor-lead-engine/index.ts`. Previously, qualification was a fragmented, sequential questionnaire where providing partial information caused loops or amnesiac re-prompting. In 26.3, buyer qualification is streamlined into 3 rapid compound turns:
1. **Turn 1 (Type & Transaction):** Captures configuration, category, and transaction type (`BUY`, `RENT`, `LEASE`).
2. **Turn 2 (Locality + Budget Band):** Resilient compound question with F-04 dynamic single-field handling (if user gives only Location, bot acknowledges location and prompts only for budget without re-asking location; if user gives only Budget, prompts only for location).
3. **Turn 3 (Timeline + Purpose):** Captures possession readiness (`READY_TO_MOVE` vs `UNDER_CONSTRUCTION`) and intent (`SELF_USE` vs `INVESTMENT` / `BUSINESS`).

Matching live inventory cards now present clean HTML hyperlinks for Google Maps and Brochures with an ultra-short 1-tap VIP Site Visit CTA. Commercial lease seekers are protected from sub-50k budget down-classification into residential rent (F-05). Existing leads with booked site visits can search for secondary properties without canceling or losing their scheduled visits (F-09). Complete qualification triggers an automatic AI lead scoring handshake elevating leads to 82+ (HOT 🔥) (F-12). All buyer templates feature natural, city-agnostic phrasing with zero occurrences of "Mitra", "sthit chhe", "shodhva mango chho", or hardcoded "Vadodara".

## Key Achievements & Implementation Details

### 1. Smart 3-Turn Compound Qualification with Dynamic Prompts (F-04 Fix)
- Restructured `handleQualificationCascade` missing-field prompts into 3 grouped compound stages:
  - **Turn 1 (`TURN_1_COMPOUND`):** If both type and transaction are missing:
    `Property Buy karni hai ya Rent par chahiye? Aur kis type ki dekh rahe hain — Flat (2/3BHK), Villa ya Shop/Office? 🏡`
  - **Turn 2 (`TURN_2_BOTH`, `TURN_2_BUDGET_ONLY`, `TURN_2_LOCATION_ONLY`):**
    - Both missing: `Noted — <b>${propType}</b>! 👍\n👉 Kaunse exact <b>Area/Landmark</b> me dekh rahe hain, aur aapka approximate <b>Budget range</b> kitna hai? 📍💰`
    - Location only given (e.g. "Bhayli me dekh rahe hain"):
      `Noted <b>${location}</b>! 📍\n👉 Aapka approximate <b>Budget range</b> kitna rahega 💰 (e.g. 50 Lakhs, 1 Cr ya 25,000/mo)?` (Zero re-prompting of location!)
    - Budget only given (e.g. "75 Lakhs budget hai"):
      `Noted budget — <b>${formatPriceShort(budget)}</b>! 💰\n👉 Aap kis preferred <b>Area ya Landmark</b> ke paas search kar rahe hain? 📍`
  - **Turn 3 (`TURN_3_BOTH`, `TURN_3_TIMELINE_ONLY`, `TURN_3_PURPOSE_ONLY`):**
    - Compound prompt: `Bahut badiya! 👍\n👉 <b>Ready to Move</b> chahiye ya Under Construction chalega? Aur <b>Family rehne ke liye</b> hai ya Investment? 🔑`
    - Enhanced `parseTimelineFromText` and `parsePurposeFromText` to map `"READY_TO_MOVE"`, `"UNDER_CONSTRUCTION"`, `"SELF_USE"`, `"INVESTMENT"`, and `"BUSINESS"`.
    - Added relaxed turn 3 cue detection ("koi bhi chalega", "dono", "options dikhao") to prevent deadlocks and advance directly to matching options.

### 2. Commercial Lease Seeker Disambiguation & Inventory Matching (F-05 & F-12 Fixes)
- **F-05 Sub-50k Budget Protection:**
  - Added commercial inquiry detection (`/(?:commercial|office|shop|showroom|godown|warehouse|retail|co-working)/i`).
  - If commercial inquiry has rental cues or sub-50k monthly budget (e.g. "office 35000 rent pe"), sets `requirement = "LEASE"` and `purpose = "BUSINESS"` instead of collapsing to residential rent.
  - Automatically sets `property_category = "COMMERCIAL"`.
- **F-05 `matchesListingType` Commercial Matching:**
  - Updated `matchesListingType` signature to accept `isCommercial?: boolean`.
  - Allowed commercial searches (`LEASE` or `RENT`) to seamlessly match inventory tagged with `LEASE`, `RENT`, or `RENTAL`.
- **Hyperlinked Property Card Presentation:**
  - Upgraded `formatN8nPropertyCard` in both commercial and residential branches to output clean HTML anchor links:
    `📍 <a href="${p.map_url}">View on Google Maps</a>` and `📄 <a href="${p.brochure_url}">Download Brochure</a>`.
  - Formatted ultra-short VIP Site Visit CTA trailer:
    `👉 <b>Kis property ki VIP Site Visit 🚗 schedule karein — Option 1 ya Option 2?</b> (Kal shaam 5:00 baje ya Sunday subah 11:00 baje?)`.
- **F-12 AI Lead Scoring Handshake:**
  - Added `hasFullQual` check in `computeEngineLeadScore` in `index.ts` and `src/lib/lead-scoring.ts`, awarding `stageScore += 35` for complete 6-attribute qualification.
  - In `handleQualificationCascade`, full qualification automatically invokes `computeEngineLeadScore(lead, "OPTIONS_SENT", leadUpdates)` and persists `lead_score` (elevated to 82+), `lead_temperature = "HOT"`, and `score_breakdown` in `leads.metadata`.

### 3. Secondary Search Visit Preservation & Gujlish Polish (F-09 Fix)
- **F-09 Secondary Search Active Visit Preservation:**
  - Added `isSecondarySearchChoice` (Option 3 / "biji property search" / "dusri property search") in `SITE_VISIT_BOOKED` state handler, reassuring user: `Bilkul <b>${nameStr}</b>! Aapki scheduled visit safe hai. Nayi property kis type ki search kar rahe hain? 🏢`.
  - In `H. NEW PROPERTY INQUIRY FROM BOOKED VISIT`, wrapped cascade execution to strictly retain `site_visit_booked: true`, preserve scheduled datetime, and maintain `SITE_VISIT_BOOKED` state without touching or canceling `site_visits` rows.
  - In `handleQualificationCascade`, guarded `state` and `site_visit_booked` when `lead.site_visit_booked === true`.
- **Gujlish Linguistic Polish:**
  - Eradicated all occurrences of "shodhva mango chho" (replaced with "joi rahya chho" or "joiye chhe").
  - Eradicated "Kharidva mango chho" (replaced with "Kharidva mate joiye chhe ke Bhade?").
  - Eradicated "Tamara budget mujab options filter karva mate" (replaced with "Tamaro andaje budget range ketlo chhe?").
  - Replaced all hardcoded "Vadodara" references in agency and office templates with dynamic city fallbacks (`context.displayCity || resolvedCity || "tamara shehar"`).
  - Maintained crisp length ergonomics (<= 3 lines per prompt).
