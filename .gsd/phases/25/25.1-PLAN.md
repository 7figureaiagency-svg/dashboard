# Plan 25.1: Owner Intake Stop-Word Guard & Entity Extraction Disambiguation

<objective>
Fix the Telegram owner listing bug where sending 'muje meri shop bechni he' caused the bot to falsely extract 'Muje meri he' as the project/society name and skip directly to location inquiry. Implement an unambiguous project/society name validation guard (`isLikelySocietyOrProjectName`), upgrade `extractProjectOrSocietyFromText` to strip property tokens and conversational filler, ensure `handleOwnerFlow` accurately acknowledges 'Commercial Shop' and queries for the Commercial Complex / Business Hub (Step 2: `OWNER_SOCIETY_PENDING`), and route first-turn owner declarations directly to `handleOwnerFlow` in `FRESH` and `ROLE_SELECTION_PENDING` states.

Purpose: Prevent conversational boilerplate from polluting project/society metadata and ensure accurate single-question cascading during owner property listing intake.
Output: Production Edge Function `v74-owner-entity-guard` and automated empirical verification test suite.
</objective>
