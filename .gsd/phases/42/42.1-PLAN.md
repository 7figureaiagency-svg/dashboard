# Plan 42.1: Pre-Visit, Arrival & Post-Visit Feedback Intent-Eliciting Templates (Modules 1A, 1, 2, 2B, 2C)

<objective>
Refactor all visit-day automated outreach templates (Morning-of-Visit Briefing, 1-Hour Pre-Visit Reminder, 1-Hour Post-Visit Feedback, Unconfirmed Attendance Nudge, and Missed Visit Reschedule) to completely eliminate passive/binary Yes/No questions. 

Replace them with active, intent-eliciting conversational prompts that encourage rich entity and slot replies (e.g. arrival status, route/landmark assistance, sample flat layout impressions, price/budget fit, or concrete 2-slot reschedule choices). Ensure 100% strict adherence to the lead's database language (`l.user_language`) across Gujlish, English, and Hinglish.
</objective>

<context>
Load for context:
- `supabase/functions/realtor-lead-engine/index.ts` (lines 3640-4105)
- `.agents/skills/cs-chatbot-design/SKILL.md` (3 Golden Response Principles, conversational ergonomics)
- `.agents/skills/realtor-lead-engine/SKILL.md` (WABA safety invariants, site visit state machine)

### Problem Definition:
1. **Module 1A Morning Briefing**:
   Currently ends with: *"Shu timing confirm samjie?"* or *"Kya hum timing confirm samjhein?"*.
   - Result: Users text back *"Haan"*, *"Na"*, *"Yes"*, *"No"*. This gives zero logistical clarity and fails to elicit reschedule slots or navigation queries.
2. **Module 1 Pre-Visit Reminder (1 hr prior)**:
   Currently ends with: *"Agar timing me thoda aage-piche ho to kripya bataiye!"*.
   - Result: Passive statement without a clear call-to-action regarding arrival status or on-site coordination.
3. **Module 2 Post-Visit Feedback (1 hr after)**:
   Currently asks: *"Were you able to attend? 1. Yes 2. No"*.
   - Result: Fails to capture qualitative feedback (e.g., liked layout, price too high, wanted different tower) or capture alternative inventory interest.
4. **Module 2B & 2C Missed/Unconfirmed Reschedule**:
   Currently asks open-ended: *"Kya hum is week aapke liye visit schedule karein?"*.
   - Result: Lacks concrete anchor slots, causing decision fatigue and low reschedule conversion.
</context>
