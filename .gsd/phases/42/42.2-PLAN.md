# Plan 42.2: Incomplete Lead Recovery & Post-Visit Nurture Drip Intent-Eliciting Templates (Modules 3 & 4)

<objective>
Refactor Module 3 (Daily Incomplete Lead Follow-up across OPTIONS_SENT, VISIT_REQUESTED, and Incomplete Qualification Cases 1-6) and Module 4 (Post-Visit Day-Wise Drip Cadence for Day 1, Day 3, and Day 7) to eliminate all passive Yes/No traps and closed questions.

### Problem Definition:
1. **Module 3 (OPTIONS_SENT)**:
   Currently ends with: *"kya aapne review keen? ... Kya hum kal shaam 5 baje VIP Site Visit plan karein?"*.
   - Result: Lead replies *"Nahi"* or *"Baad me dekhenge"*, shutting down the dialogue without specifying what was wrong with the options.
2. **Module 3 (Incomplete Qualification)**:
   Currently asks: *"Aapka budget range bata dijiye"*.
   - Result: Without concrete anchor examples, leads often hesitate or text vague replies like *"Thik thak budget hai"*.
3. **Module 4 (Day 1, Day 3, Day 7 Drips)**:
   - Day 1 asks: *"Kya hum booking process share karein?"* -> closed Yes/No.
   - Day 3 asks: *"Kya hum EMI calculation assist karein?"* -> closed Yes/No.
   - Day 7 asks: *"Kya hum developer ke sath final meeting arrange karein?"* -> closed Yes/No.
   - Result: Closed questions trigger high friction and low conversion.
</context>
