# Phase 5 Plan 5.1 Summary: Role-Based Executive Routing, Admin Listing Approvals, and Manager Unassigned Lead Triage

## 1. Executive Summary

Implemented enterprise 3-tier Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) and automated lead/alert routing across Nextgn Realtors CRM and Supabase Edge Function:
1. **Admin / Principal Desk (`role = 'admin'`)**:
   - Sole authority to review title documents, verify paperwork, and approve owner submissions (`property_submissions`) into live inventory.
   - Directly receives all `NEW_OWNER_LISTING` alerts on Telegram/WhatsApp.
   - Owner leads are automatically assigned to the Admin executive (`assigned_executive_id = adminExec.id`).
2. **Manager / Operations Desk (`role = 'manager'`)**:
   - Oversees team pipeline and off-market triage.
   - Automatically receives all unassigned buyer inquiries and `NO_PROPERTY_MATCH` alerts.
   - Leads matching no direct inventory are assigned to the Manager for manual sourcing and executive re-delegation.
3. **Sales Executives / Specialists (`role = 'sales_executive'`)**:
   - Receive qualified site visits (`SITE_VISIT_BOOKED`), negotiation, and feedback alerts for their assigned properties.
   - Scoped view in CRM (`myLeads`).
   - Strictly prohibited from approving property submissions into live inventory (approval buttons disabled with explanatory tooltips, toast warnings, and Restricted Mode banner).
