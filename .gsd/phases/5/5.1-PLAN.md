# Plan 5.1: Role-Based Executive Routing, Admin Listing Approvals, and Manager Unassigned Lead Triage

<objective>
Implement role-based lead routing and permission boundaries across the Edge Function and CRM Dashboard:
1. Owner property submissions (`property_submissions` & `NEW_OWNER_LISTING`) must route exclusively to and be assigned to the `admin` executive role, with approval rights restricted exclusively to Admins.
2. Unassigned buyer leads (e.g. `NO_PROPERTY_MATCH` or fresh inquiries without property matches) must route and be assigned to the `manager` executive role for triage.
3. Sales executives receive qualified buyer site visit and negotiation alerts, but are strictly prohibited from approving property listings into live inventory.

Purpose: Prevent unauthorized listing approvals by sales agents and establish clear operational separation between Admin (Inventory & Compliance), Manager (Triage & Off-market), and Sales (Visits & Conversion).
Output: Updated CRM UI modals, Edge Function alert & assignment logic, and end-to-end verification script.
</objective>
