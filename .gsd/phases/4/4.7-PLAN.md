# Plan 4.7: Past Site Visit Temporal Greeting Guards & Missed Visit Cron Follow-up

<objective>
Fix two critical temporal real estate lifecycle bugs reported for Telegram lead 5117510624:
1. **Past Site Visit Labeled as 'Upcoming' in Greetings**: When a site visit scheduled time has passed (e.g., visit was 10:00 AM, user says 'Hi' at 2:14 PM), the bot was blindly outputting `🚗 Upcoming Site Visit: Pearl Heights ke liye scheduled hai at 22 Oct 2026, 10:00 am.` because `hasActiveVisit` checked boolean truthiness of `lead.site_visit_at` (a persistent DB string) rather than checking if the visit is active AND in the future (`scheduled_at > now`).
2. **Missing Post-Visit / Missed-Visit Automated Follow-Up**: The cron sweep Module 2 filtered strictly on `site_visit_booked = true` and Module 2B explicitly excluded `VISIT_MISSED`, leaving leads who missed or cancelled their visit stranded without an automated rescheduling follow-up.
</objective>

<context>
- User report for Telegram lead `5117510624` (Manthan):
  `Welcome back Manthan ji! Nextgen Realty desk par aapka swagat hai. 🙏✨`
  `🚗 Upcoming Site Visit: Pearl Heights ke liye scheduled hai at 22 Oct 2026, 10:00 am.`
  `📋 Listed Property: Spring field (Status: Pending Verification / Under Review).`
  Sent at 2:14 PM IST for a 10:00 AM IST visit that already passed.
- Relevant code locations in `supabase/functions/realtor-lead-engine/index.ts`:
  1. **Dual-Role Greeting Generator (~lines 3953–4025)**:
     - Currently: `const hasActiveVisit = Boolean(activeVisit || lead.site_visit_at || lead.site_visit_datetime || lead.site_visit_booked);`
     - Evaluates to `true` forever because `lead.site_visit_at` is an ISO timestamp string from the past!
     - Need temporal classification:
       - `isUpcomingVisit = activeVisit && lead.site_visit_booked && (new Date(visitTime).getTime() > Date.now());`
       - `isPastUnconfirmedVisit = activeVisit && (activeVisit.status === 'SCHEDULED' || activeVisit.status === 'CONFIRMED') && (new Date(visitTime).getTime() <= Date.now());`
       - `isMissedVisit = lead.state === 'VISIT_MISSED' || activeVisit?.status === 'MISSED';`
  2. **SITE_VISIT_BOOKED Greeting (~lines 4824–4860)**:
     - Checks if `scheduledTime` is in the past; if so, prompts for attendance confirmation / reschedule instead of saying "Milte hain site par!".
  3. **Cron Sweep Module 2 & 2B (~lines 2624–2740)**:
     - Module 2: Check for unconfirmed past visits via `site_visits` table join or checking past visits where `status === 'SCHEDULED'` and `scheduled_at` passed > 45 mins ago.
     - Module 2C: Add follow-up sweep for `state === 'VISIT_MISSED'` leads between 2 hours and 48 hours after miss, prompting them to reschedule.
</context>
