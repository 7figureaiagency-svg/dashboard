# Plan 4.8 Summary: In-Drawer Persona Switcher, 1-Tap Manual Owner Listing Studio & Dual-Source Property Matchmaker

## 1. Overview & Objectives
Plan 4.8 bridges the operational gap between WhatsApp bot seller workflows and the CRM executive workstation:
- **Apple HIG Persona Switcher**: Sales reps can seamlessly toggle any lead between `Buyer (Seeker)`, `Seller (Provider)`, and `Dual Role 🌟` with 1 tap.
- **1-Tap Manual Owner Listing Studio**: Executives taking resale calls or walk-ins can directly submit property listings for clients into `property_submissions` without needing the client to text the bot.
- **Dual-Source Property Matchmaker**: The lead drawer's matching engine now simultaneously aggregates developer inventory units and verified owner resale listings, badging resale units distinctly (`[Verified Resale 🔑]`) and offering a 1-tap WhatsApp Owner outreach link.
