# Plan 4.9: In-House Buyer ↔ Seller Cross-Matchmaker & Natural "Buyer Inquiry" Bot Intent Engine

<objective>
Turn the Nextgn Realtors platform into an autonomous in-house brokerage engine:
1. In `OwnerSubmissionsView` (`inventory-tab.tsx`), cross-match every submitted property against active buyer leads in the database, surfacing double-ended commission opportunities (`👥 N Matching In-House Buyers`).
2. Add a 1-tap "Share Resale Pitch via WhatsApp" action to immediately connect verified sellers' properties to matching buyers.
3. In `supabase/functions/realtor-lead-engine/index.ts`, recognize natural seller inquiries ("Koi buyer mila?", "Mere flat ke liye koi inquiry aayi?") and reply with real-time, authentic buyer counts and demand metrics from the live database.
</objective>

<context>
- Plan 4.8 provides the manual listing studio and dual-source matcher in `LeadDrawer`.
- Resale properties submitted by local owners have high margins. Connecting existing buyer leads with existing seller listings creates zero-inventory, high-profit internal transactions.
- Currently, if an owner asks the bot "Koi buyer mila?", the bot gives a generic status message rather than checking if any buyers in the CRM actually match their property.
- Multi-Tenant Isolation Invariant: Matchmaking must be strictly partitioned by `client_id` (Griham buyers only match Griham sellers).
</context>
