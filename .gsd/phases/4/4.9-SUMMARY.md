# Plan 4.9 Summary: In-House Buyer ↔ Seller Cross-Matchmaker & Natural "Buyer Inquiry" Bot Intent Engine

## 1. Overview & Objectives
Plan 4.9 transforms Nextgn Realtors CRM into an autonomous in-house brokerage and cross-matching engine:
1. **In-House Cross-Matchmaker Studio**: In `OwnerSubmissionsView` (`src/components/crm/inventory-tab.tsx`), every resale property submitted by an owner is dynamically cross-matched against active in-house buyer leads in the database based on tenant partition (`client_id`), location proximity, configuration matching, and budget viability (buyer budget >= 80% expected price).
2. **Matching Buyers Slide-over Sheet**: Sales reps can click the Apple HIG `{count} In-House Buyers Matching` badge on any submission card/row to open an inspector sheet with buyer cards, real-time `LeadScoreBadge` temperatures, and a 1-tap **"Pitch Resale via WhatsApp"** CTA with pre-formatted consultative pitch text.
3. **Natural "Buyer Mila Kya?" Bot Intent Handler**: In `supabase/functions/realtor-lead-engine/index.ts`, when a property seller or dual-role user inquires over WhatsApp/Telegram about buyer demand (e.g. *"Mere flat ke liye koi buyer mila kya?"*, *"Any inquiries received?"*), the conversational bot queries live CRM buyer leads and replies with grounded matching metrics and budget brackets without hallucinations.
