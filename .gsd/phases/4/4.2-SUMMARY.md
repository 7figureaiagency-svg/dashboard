# Phase 4 Plan 4.2 Summary: CRM Dashboard Dual-Role Badges, Drawer Sub-Tabs & High-Value Sales Alerts

## Objective
Extend the Nextgen Realtors Apple HIG CRM Dashboard with visual transparency for multi-persona clients. Deliver distinguished `[🌟 DUAL: BUYER + SELLER]` badges in lead lists and pipeline board stages, a dedicated "Seller Submissions" sub-view inside the Lead Detail Drawer alongside the Buyer Profile, and actionable sales pitch hints for senior executives handling dual-role transactions.

## Changes Made

1. **`src/lib/crm.ts`**:
   - Added `lead_type?: string | null` to the hydrated `Lead` interface.
   - Updated `PROPERTY_STATE_MAP` with `DUAL_ROLE_ACTIVE` and `DUAL_ROLE_MENU_PENDING` (royal purple tone).
   - Updated `STATUS_MAP` with `DUAL_ROLE` (`"Dual: Buyer + Seller 🌟"`) and `DUAL_ROLE_MENU_PENDING`.
   - Exported helper function `isDualRoleLead(lead?: Lead | null): boolean` to recognize dual-role clients across lead_type, status, state, and metadata combinations.
   - Enhanced `hydrateLead` to hydrate `lead_type` seamlessly.

2. **`src/components/crm/badges.tsx`**:
   - Implemented and exported `DualRoleBadge` component adhering to Apple HIG: royal purple translucent pill with hairline border, amber star icon, and `[🌟 DUAL: BUYER + SELLER]` typography.

3. **`src/components/crm/leads-tab.tsx`**:
   - Added `DUAL_ROLE` triage pill widget into the complication row (`Dual Clients 🌟`, showing count of dual leads).
   - Added `"DUAL_ROLE"` filter option in `STATUS_FILTER_OPTIONS`.
   - Integrated `DualRoleBadge` into:
     - Pipeline Board cards (under customer name/budget)
     - Mobile responsive cards (under client title)
     - Desktop table view (below client name)
   - Updated quick filter banner to highlight `"Dual Role Clients (Buyer + Seller 🌟)"`.

4. **`src/components/crm/lead-drawer.tsx`**:
   - Integrated `usePropertySubmissions` hook to query seller submissions tied to `lead.lead_id` or owner phone number.
   - Added `🌟 High-Value Dual Client: Active Property Seeker + Verified Property Seller` alert card at top of drawer with senior executive pitch hint: *"Client is actively looking to buy while listing their property. Prioritize senior partner callback for simultaneous exchange or double commission closure."*
   - Added dynamic Seller Listings tab (`<TabsTrigger value="seller"><Building2 className="size-3.5" /> Seller ({submissions.length})</TabsTrigger>`).
   - Implemented `<TabsContent value="seller">` rendering Apple Inset-Grouped cards for each submitted property with:
     - Society / Project name & Locality
     - Configuration, Area (carpet / built-up), and expected price/rent
     - Furnishing & Possession status
     - Verification badge (`Pending Verification`, `Verified`, `Rejected`)
     - 44pt touch target Call Owner and WhatsApp Owner inspection action buttons.

5. **`src/components/crm/whatsapp-chat-view.tsx`**:
   - Added `[🌟 DUAL]` header badge for dual-role leads.
   - Styled `DUAL_ROLE_MENU_PENDING` bubble badge with royal purple highlights.
