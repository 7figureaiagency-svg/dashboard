# Real Estate Copywriting Frameworks & Pitch Templates

## 1. High-Converting Pitch Frameworks

### A. The Hook-Story-Offer (HSO) WhatsApp Framework
1. **Hook (Line 1):** Pattern interrupt with high-relevance localization (Society name + BHK).
2. **Story/Specs (Lines 2-4):** 3 punchy bullet points emphasizing the buyer's primary archetype motivator (space, yield, status, or EMI).
3. **Offer/CTA (Last Line):** Low-friction VIP Site Visit invitation with 2 time slot options.
